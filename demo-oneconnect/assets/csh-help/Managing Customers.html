Managing Customers

Customer Management

In OneConnect, a Customer is an organization that is managed by an Allied Telesis Partner.

A OneConnect user with the PartnerAdmin privilege level can perform all management tasks for all Customers, while a user with the PartnerUser privilege level can perform some management tasks for Customers to which they are given access or linked.

This table provides a summary of supported customer management tasks and which Partner users can perform each task.

Table 1. Customer Management Tasks
TasksWho Can Perform Tasks
Add a New CustomerPartnerAdmins, PartnerUsers
Add Customer SitesPartnerAdmins, PartnerUsers
Enable Customer Access for PartnerUsersPartnerAdmins
Allocate Device Licenses to a CustomerPartnerAdmins

Add a New Customer

This topic explains how a PartnerAdmin or a PartnerUser can add a new Customer to OneConnect.

  1. In the left pane, click Customer Management.
    The Customers page will appear.
  2. In the Customers page, click + New Customer in the upper right corner.
    The New Customer dialog will appear.
  3. Add the customer’s details and any notes, then click Save.
  4. Back in the Customers page, verify that the new customer now appears in the table.
  5. If you need to change any of the customer’s details, click the Actions icon associated with the customer, and from the drop-down list that appears, select Edit.
    The Edit Customer dialog will appear.
  6. Make the desired changes, and click Save.

Add Customer Sites

After a Customer is added to OneConnect, a PartnerAdmin or a PartnerUser can add one or more sites for the Customer. Once Customer sites are added, a PartnerAdmin or PartnerUser can select a site when adding a device for the Customer. Sites provide another way to organize, monitor, and manage Customer devices.

To add a Customer site:

  1. In the left pane, click Customer Management.
    The Customers page will appear.
  2. In the Customers table, click the name of the customer, for example, Customer One, to which you want to add one or more sites.
    The customer page will appear.
  3. In the customer page, click + New Site in the upper right corner.
    The New Site dialog will appear.
  4. Enter the site details, and click Save.
  5. Back in the customer page, click the Sites tab and verify that the new site appears in the table.

Enable Customer Access for PartnerUsers

These Customer access rules apply to the Partner users:

  • A PartnerAdmin can access all Customers added for a Partner.
  • A PartnerUser can access a Customer only if they have added the customer to OneConnect.

This topic explains how a PartnerAdmin can enable access for PartnerUsers who didn’t add a particular Customer to OneConnect but still need access.

To enable a PartnerUser to access to a customer:

  1. In the left pane, click Customer Management.
    The Customers page will appear.
  2. In the Customers table, find the name of the Customer that you want the PartnerUser to access, for example, Customer One. Click the associated Actions icon, and from the drop-down list that appears, select Link Users.
    The Link Users dialog will display PartnerUsers that don’t currently have access to the Customer.
  3. Select the user for which you want to enable access, then click Save.
    A confirmation message will appear at the bottom of the screen.
