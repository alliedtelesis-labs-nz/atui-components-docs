What you can ask the assistant

Request Information About Network Devices

Information Request Example

For example, you can ask the AI Network Assistant to check the firmware version running on the devices in your network. If your devices are organized into sites, the results are grouped by Site to make them easier to review. This organization can help you quickly understand what is on your network, especially in larger environments or where manual inventory checks would otherwise take a significant amount of time.

Sample Question and Request

Sample questions/requests include:

  • Give me the list of firmware-manageable devices on my network
  • What firmware are the devices running?

Request a Summary of Recent Events

Summary Request Example

In return, the AI Network Assistant presents the summary along with the time that each event occurred. Accessing the summary helps you to quickly identify recent critical issues and reduces the effort required to review event activity manually.

Sample Question and Request

Sample questions/requests include:

  • What are the key takeaways from my recent events?
  • Summarize my recent events

Upgrade Firmware on Network Devices

Before you begin this task, you must upload the desired firmware files to the AMF Master device that manages the area in which the devices with outdated firmware reside.

In return, the AI Network Assistant checks the current firmware files, and distributes the desired firmware files from an AMF Master device to your network devices. The AI Network Assistant’s upgrade process includes confirmation steps that enable you to verify the changes before they are applied.

Sample Question and Request

Sample questions/requests include:

  • Upgrade the firmware on <device>
  • Upgrade the firmware on <device> to <firmware_version>
  1. Access the AI Network Assistant by clicking the associated icon in the upper right corner of any page.
  2. Ask the AI Network Assistant to upgrade firmware on a specified network to a version stored on the AMF master device.
    The AI Network Assistant will display all available versions of the firmware to which you can upgrade the device.
  3. Review the firmware version or versions identified by the AI Network Assistant, and verify that they are correct before proceeding.
  4. After the AI Network Assistant acknowledges your selection, click Confirm to proceed with the upgrade.
    During the upgrade process, the assistant will display the status. If needed, you can stop it by clicking Stop.
