How device licenses work

Device License Management

Each Allied Telesis device that is onboarded through OneConnect must have a license. Licenses are available for 1, 10, 100 or 1,000 devices for a time period of 1 year.

Typically, an Allied Telesis Partner buys licenses to cover all customers that they manage. After buying the licenses, the Partner receives an email with the license details, including a license file and license key.

Note: An Allied Telesis Administrator can also perform the license management tasks included in the table. Before starting, we recommend that the PartnerAdmin checks with the Allied Telesis Administrator to ensure that there is no duplication of effort.
Table 1. Workflow of New License Management Tasks
SequenceTask
1Add the new licenses to OneConnect
2Learn about different license management options, and apply one of them if desired
3Allocate licenses to a customer as needed
4After a customer device is added to OneConnect, add a license to the device
Note: As soon as device licenses are added to OneConnect, the expiry date countdown will begin.
